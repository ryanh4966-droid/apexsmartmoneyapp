Features that make money management feel simple.

Apex Smart Money App combines clean design with powerful automation so you can stop guessing and start deciding.

Core features

Unified dashboard

See balances, spending, and upcoming bills in one place. No more logging into five different apps.

Smart categories

Transactions are auto-grouped into clear categories so you instantly see where your money actually goes.

Goal tracking

Create savings goals, debt payoff plans, or emergency funds and track progress in real time.

Automation that works for you

Rules, not routines

Set simple rules like “move 10% of every paycheck to savings” or “alert me when dining out passes $200 this month.”

Once your rules are set, Apex quietly monitors and nudges you when something needs attention.

Examples of smart rules

  • Notify me when a subscription price changes
  • Warn me 3 days before any bill over $200
  • Round up purchases and send the difference to savings
